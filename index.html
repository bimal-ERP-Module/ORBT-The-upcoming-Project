Career Guidance & Awareness Initiative03
The opportunity

Why this initiative matters

A student's career direction is often shaped by the information available around them—not always by the full range of opportunities.

Limited awareness

Many students know familiar routes, but may not know the range of academic, technical and skill-based pathways.

Unequal access

Students in rural areas and smaller towns may have less access to career counselling, industry exposure and reliable information.

High-stakes choices

Course and career decisions affect time, finances and future opportunities. Better information can reduce avoidable confusion.

Make useful career information easier to understand, access and act on.
